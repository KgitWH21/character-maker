Make it yours

The guided builder

Start with Who are they?, What do they want?, and What gets in their way?. Each possibility combines real character-array entries. Choose See ingredients to edit, choose or reroll an element, change an influence pool, or hold part of the sentence. Add to character keeps the sentence and its source details. Try another preserves kept facts and locked or held elements. Write my own lets you author the whole sentence. Skip any question and return whenever you like.

Character holds your sketch, Story tools opens writing exercises, and Library holds saved versions and backups. Browse all details opens every original generator and the single-detail builder. Preview & export includes your composed sentences and kept details.

Roll character changes unlocked results. Shuffle focus only changes which fields stand out.

Tap or click a field’s label, value, or card background to toggle its focus. Buttons and editing controls keep their own actions. You can also use the keyboard-accessible Focus button. Use Copy on any field to paste its label as a heading and its text as a paragraph into Google Docs. Every field has Focus, Lock, Keep, and Reroll. Keep adds it to My Sheet. Lock preserves it on future rolls. Open Edit to choose an original entry or write your own.

Build a Character, Quick Snapshot, and every deeper panel share the same character. Switching views won't reroll anything.

Build a Character

Start with a compact identity foundation, then tap Add characteristic. A candidate shows an existing background result. Keep accepts a snapshot for the canvas and My Sheet. Another changes the proposed field within your selected category; Reroll Value rolls that same field using its existing dependency rules. Dismiss adds nothing. Surprise me gives eligible categories equal chances before choosing a field.

Suggestions prefer unaccepted fields in focus and avoid repeats within a cycle. When only faded fields remain, choose Shuffle focus or Include remaining faded. Focus determines suggestions; acceptance determines canvas content. Accepted cards remain readable after focus changes. Explore section opens the relevant field on the same character; the sticky Back to Character button returns to your canvas. Keep in any panel also adds that detail to the canvas.

Canvas Edit changes the accepted snapshot. Replace with current result deliberately captures the latest background value. Roll character preserves kept snapshots and locked background values. New character, in Actions, creates a fresh draft and asks before discarding accepted details or notes; saved snapshots remain available. Quick Snapshot retains the existing customizable compact assortment. Use Explore / tools for all deeper panels, My Sheet, and Saved characters. A second Actions menu stays at the bottom right as you scroll, with the same Roll character, Shuffle focus, and New character controls.

Dialogue Style

Dialogue Style gives concrete writing directions for amount, sentence shape, register, words, profanity, speech and messaging presentation, multilingual behavior, conversational habits, thought-to-speech, omissions, and situational changes. The original acoustic descriptions remain under Voice Sound. These are options to curate, not habits to apply to every sentence. Examples demonstrate individual options and are not one conversation.

Keep captures a detail for My Sheet and the Dialogue recipe. The recipe includes only kept dialogue details and Voice Sound, including their earlier snapshots after rerolls. Copy recipe copies those choices. New fields are available in Choose snapshot fields without being added to the default sketch.

Optional Dialogue Wildcards starts with one writing challenge. Combine two avoids declared incompatible pairs on random rolls. Manual choices and locks are preserved; conflicting selections are explained. Focus on a wildcard is a suggestion, never enforcement. Edit a challenge to choose its scope, parameters, or fictional reason. English letter, word, and syllable conventions are labeled; syllable and grammar examples were checked manually.

Main language follows National heritage using an editable country default. Second and third languages are distinct random languages, or None. Unlocked full rolls produce 55% one language, 32% two, and 13% three. Locked values and accepted snapshots are preserved. Multilingual cards use all three language fields as their reference; Edit permits a manual override. Switching requires author-established ability in another language. Bracketed examples are fillable templates. A wildcard’s English–Spanish example illustrates one switch without assigning either language to the character. Situational change names one baseline habit and proposes an exception under anger, fear, comfort, or authority.

Expand the named arrays in dialogue-data.js. Each option has an ID, label, instruction, and example. Wildcard compatibility exclusions and parameter pools are editable there; local generation logic is in dialogue.js.

Right Now

A moment in the same character’s life: place, activity, private thoughts, fictional device readings, media, conversation, sightings, stated status, and immediate context. Each slot uses the same Focus, Lock, Keep, Edit, and Reroll controls. Quick Snapshot can include any of these same values.

Footprint archetypes change the odds of available sources on future rolls, without defining personality. No source, Unknown, Unavailable, Outdated, Concealed, and Conflicting are usable information states. They are separate from random focus, which changes emphasis only. Faded fields stay readable and interactive.

Known to whom? changes presentation without deleting values. Observer views mark private experience unavailable; sightings separate recorded behavior from interpretation. Edit / choose · author details exposes the underlying author value, even when a source lacks it. My Sheet retains exactly what you kept, including earlier author details.

Connected moment coordinates setting, spot, activity, partner/method, and goal/next action. Parent rerolls can update unlocked dependent slots. Locked conflicts remain for you to curate. Independent mode allows surprising combinations. Device readings always require an available fictional device; retained locked readings are not presented as current readings when it is absent.

All telemetry, communications, sightings, and statuses are invented locally. This app never accesses sensors, health data, location services, or accounts. Expand named arrays and probability rules in right-now-data.js; field definitions and lightweight relationships are in right-now.js.

Undo & roll history

Undo and Redo are in both Actions menus. Use Roll history to restore a complete earlier character or field roll. Recent changes include edits, locks, kept details, notes, and workshop tools. Ctrl/Cmd+Z and Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z work outside text editors. Recent history survives reloading on this device; save snapshots for permanent versions. Undo changes the working draft, while saved characters stay separate.

Connected characters

Generate a sibling, rival, ally, coworker, friend, or mentor, or use an existing saved character. Each has their own profile, shared history, two perspectives, and a current tension. Expand the relationship to edit it. Save current & open preserves your current draft as a saved snapshot before opening the connected profile. Copies are independent of saved sources.

Put them under pressure

Generate a dilemma based on kept traits, motivations, flaws, and goals. Write the response and Keep situation to retain it. Another situation preserves kept scenes. Reactions remain your choice.

Finished character brief

Review a printable brief made from the identity foundation and kept snapshots. Add a one-line introduction and an optional PNG, JPG, or WebP portrait up to 250 KB. Choose all kept details or a compact reference, and include notes, relationships, and kept situations. Copy brief, Print / Save PDF, and Download HTML use the same reviewed content. Long briefs flow onto additional pages.

Medical

Medical shares the current character, with precise height in feet/inches, total inches, and centimeters; weight in pounds and kilograms; a calculated weight category; condition and medication prompts; last food, elapsed hours, and their reaction. Edit numeric fields in your chosen unit. Unlocked height details follow each other; locked conflicts remain visible for review. Keep captures snapshots, so later changes do not replace accepted details.

Adult human BMI reference categories apply only from confirmed age 20. Younger characters and other species remain unclassified. Medication prompts include common and rare conditions, plus none and unknown; they are modern fictional references without doses, not treatment plans. Set a custom condition and medications in Edit for other histories or story eras.

Education & Training

Browse all details opens education level, degree subjects, current enrollment, trade training, and feelings about education. Completed subjects follow the overall level; edit or lock fields to author an unusual path.

Relationship details

Explore relationship history, status, durations, children, traits, viewpoints, and F-to-S ratings. Overall rating averages the available areas. Current status draws from romantic entries in the original family_status pool. Sexual-history, intimacy, and attractiveness fields require a confirmed adult age. Teenagers receive nonsexual relationship prompts; children do not receive this category.

Birthday and age

Enter a story year to see the age they turn that year. Add an exact story date to see their age on that day. Edit the birthday in its card. Western zodiac uses common date ranges; cusp dates can vary. Chinese zodiac uses Lunar New Year and the year’s heavenly-stem element. Symbolic associations are prompts for your writing.

Install on your phone

Checking update availability…

iPhone / iPad: open your GitHub Pages link in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Enable Open as Web App if offered.

Android: open the link in Chrome, then tap Install app or choose Install / Add to Home screen from the browser menu.

Open the hosted app once while online. It downloads its files for offline use. A ZIP file itself doesn't install as a phone app; upload its contents to GitHub Pages first.

Update an installed app: open the app online and tap Check updates at the top of the installed app, in this help panel, or at the bottom of the page. When App update ready appears, apply any open field edits, then tap Update & reload. Your draft is saved before reloading, and your saved characters stay on this device. No reinstall or clearing browser data is needed. If an older version still shows Install app, close it from Recent apps and reopen it online after the update has downloaded.

Put it on GitHub

Download the ZIP below and extract it. Upload the contents of the character-maker folder to your repository root. In Settings → Pages, select Deploy from a branch, main, / (root). No build step is needed. The README includes the steps.

Saved characters stay in this browser on this device. Export a backup before changing devices or clearing browser data.
